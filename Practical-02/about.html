About StudentHub Portal

StudentHub was conceived and developed as part of the Web Development Frameworks (ITUE203) curriculum at the Faculty of Technology & Engineering (FTE), CHARUSAT.

Our Core Objectives

  • Consolidated Information: Deliver real-time notifications and departmental updates directly to students.
  • Frictionless Event Registration: Enable students to discover events, register with one click, and avoid paper queues.
  • Transparent Governance: Provide faculty coordinators and administrators with comprehensive oversight, audit trails, and data export.
  • Universal Accessibility: Maintain strict WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility compliance across all interactive components.

Participating Departments

CSPIT - Information Technology
