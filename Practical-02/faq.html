Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Find answers regarding account registration, event bookings, accessibility features, and student services.

1. Account & Access Queries

How do I register for an account on StudentHub?

Navigate to the Registration Page, supply your legal full name, verified CHARUSAT student email address (e.g. 23it001@charusat.edu.in), 10-digit mobile number, select your department, and create a strong password conforming to university security policies.

What should I do if I forget my password?

Visit the Login Page and select the "Forgot Password" link. Alternatively, submit a support ticket via the Contact Support Form with your university roll number and identity verification.

How does the "Remember Me" session security token work?

When selected, StudentHub issues a cryptographically random, 64-character token stored in an HTTP-only cookie. Its cryptographic SHA-256 hash is verified against the database upon your next visit, extending access securely for 30 days without exposing plaintext tokens.

2. Events & Registration Queries

How do I secure a seat for workshops or hackathons?

Go to the Events Catalog, locate your desired event, and click "Register Seat". If seats are available, your reservation will immediately reflect in your Student Dashboard.

Can I cancel an event booking if my schedule conflicts?

Yes. In your Student Dashboard, expand your active registrations table and choose "Cancel Registration" at least 24 hours prior to event commencement to release the seat for waitlisted peers.

Where can I download my participation certificates?

Certificates are issued electronically after attendance is verified by event organizers. You can download digitally signed PDF certificates directly from your Student Profile.
